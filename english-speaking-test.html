Diagnostic Test

English Speaking Test

There are two parts: you'll listen to and read a short passage aloud, then answer a question in your own words. This test needs access to your microphone and speakers.

Part 1 · Read Aloud

Listen, then read the passage aloud

Every morning, Maya walks her dog around the park before school. She stops by the small bakery on the corner, where the owner always saves her a warm roll. By the time she reaches her classroom, she is ready to start the day.

Listen to the passage, then record yourself reading it aloud from start to finish.

Part 2 · Free Speaking

Answer the question in your own words

Describe a typical day in your life.

Speak for about 30–60 seconds. There's no wrong answer — we're listening to how you express it.
